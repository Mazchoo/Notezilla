
## Quiz

Quizzes are multiple choice questions where each choice should either be selected or left unselected to be in a correct state. They are usually added at the end of chapters so the reader can check their understanding.

There is a reveal button for each question, and when it is revealed, correct answers are formatted differently to incorrect answers. Answers that have a `+` as the first character are expected to be selected, and ones with `-` are expected not to be selected. Answers will show an explanation when the expected answer is selected, and optionally an explanation when the expected one is not selected.

Lock the document to try the quiz out.

```quiz
This is a question
Answer A
  + Explanation of why it is correct
Answer B
  + When selected (correct)
  When not selected
Answer C
  - Explanation why it is wrong
Answer D
  - Not selected, correct
  Explanation when selected

This is another question
Answer A
  - Explanation of why it is correct when not selected
Answer B
  + When selected (correct)
```

## Flash cards

Flash cards are a collection of cards used for memorisation and quick review. It is well established in cognitive psychology that repeated sessions over a period of time lead to better retention.

Each flashcard is just one line with the question and one line with the answer. When viewing the flash cards, there is a reveal button that shows the answer. Since the output format is just plain HTML and CSS, the flash cards are just shown in sequence. There are next and previous buttons for navigation.

```flashcards
What is the capital of Peru?
Lima

How many grams to a Baht in a Thai gold bar?\
15.244 grams

What percentage of the Earth's crust is sedimentary rock by volume?
~8% and ~65% of continental crust exposure 
```
